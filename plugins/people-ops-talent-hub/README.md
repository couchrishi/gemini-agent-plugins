# People Ops, Talent Calibration & Workforce Analytics (`people-ops-talent-hub`)

Calibrates structured interview scorecards for score dispersion and bias signals, orchestrates 30/60/90 onboarding plans with mandatory Day-1 gate checks, and audits effective-dated Workday compensation bands with FTE/CWK separation and small-cell privacy redaction.

## Included Workflow Skills

1. **`structured-hiring-rubric`** ([SKILL.md](skills/structured-hiring-rubric/SKILL.md)) — Audits 1-4 interview scorecards for >=2.0 dispersion, thin behavioral evidence (<40 chars), and protected-class bias signals.
2. **`employee-onboarding-navigator`** ([SKILL.md](skills/employee-onboarding-navigator/SKILL.md)) — Tracks 30/60/90 onboarding milestones, enforces mandatory Day-1 IT/security gates, and detects blocked upstream dependencies.
3. **`workforce-attrition-comp-audit`** ([SKILL.md](skills/workforce-attrition-comp-audit/SKILL.md)) — Deduplicates effective-dated HRIS rows, isolates CWKs from FTEs, normalizes hourly/monthly/FX pay, and suppresses <5 privacy cells.
